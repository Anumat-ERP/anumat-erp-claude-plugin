# Warm-up checklist (about 15 minutes)

Details and the research behind each exercise:
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/demo-storytelling/reference/warmup.md`. Finish no more than 10 minutes before the slot.

## Breathing (0:00 to 3:00)
- [ ] Box breathing: in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Five rounds
- [ ] Physiological sigh: two inhales through the nose, one long exhale. Three times

## Body (3:00 to 6:00)
- [ ] Shoulder rolls: 10 back, 10 forward; lift to ears, hold, drop
- [ ] Neck: ear to shoulder, each side
- [ ] Jaw and face: wide open, then scrunch, three times
- [ ] Shake out hands, 10 seconds
- [ ] Grounding: feet hip-width, knees soft
- [ ] Power stance, 1 to 2 minutes (it may help you feel steady; the hormone claims did not replicate)

## Voice (6:00 to 11:00)
- [ ] Lip trills, sliding low to high, 30 seconds
- [ ] Humming on "mmm", sliding through the range, 30 seconds
- [ ] Sirens on "ng", three times
- [ ] Tongue twisters, slow then at pace: "Red leather, yellow leather" · "Unique New York" · "The tip of the tongue, the teeth, the lips"
- [ ] Project the opening line to the back wall, three times; then once at normal volume
- [ ] Sip room-temperature water

## Mind (11:00 to 15:00)
- [ ] Visualise the first 30 seconds, twice, in real time
- [ ] Say the first two lines aloud, three times
- [ ] Say "I'm excited"
- [ ] Pick one delivery goal: ____________

## Walking on
- [ ] Phone silent
- [ ] Cue card in hand
- [ ] One physiological sigh
