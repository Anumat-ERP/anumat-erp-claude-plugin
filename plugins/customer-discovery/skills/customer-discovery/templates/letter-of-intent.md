# Letter of intent outline

A short, usually non-binding letter in which a buyer states they intend to buy
if you deliver something specific. Stronger than words, weaker than money. Not
legal advice: have a lawyer review it, especially the binding clauses. Context:
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/customer-discovery/reference/experiments.md`.

---

**[Customer letterhead or name]**
Date: ____

To: [your company]

**Re: Intent to purchase [product / service]**

1. **Need.** [Customer] currently faces [problem, in their words], which costs
   us approximately [money / hours] per [period].

2. **What we intend to buy.** Subject to the conditions below, [customer]
   intends to purchase [product / service] covering [scope: users, sites,
   volume].

3. **Conditions.** This intent depends on [your company] delivering:
   - [specific capability 1];
   - [specific capability 2];
   - by [date].

4. **Price.** The expected price is [amount] per [period / unit], [with / without]
   a founding-customer discount of [x].

5. **Next step.** If the conditions are met, we expect to sign a purchase
   agreement within [n] weeks of delivery.

6. **Non-binding.** This letter expresses intent only and does not create a
   binding obligation to purchase, except for [any binding clauses, e.g.
   confidentiality].

Signed: ____
Name and role: ____ (ideally the person who controls the budget)

---

## Checklist before counting it as signal

- [ ] Signed by someone with budget authority, not only a friendly user
- [ ] States a price
- [ ] States specific conditions and a date
- [ ] Customer is in the ICP
