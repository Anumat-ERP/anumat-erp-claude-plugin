# Outreach message templates

Short, specific, honest. Ask for learning, never a sale. Replace every bracket.
Channel guidance: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/customer-discovery/reference/finding-people.md`.
Do not claim a product exists if it does not.

## 1. Warm intro request (to someone you know)

> Hi [name], I'm looking into how [role, e.g. clinic managers] handle
> [problem, e.g. rescheduling no-shows]. We haven't built anything; I just want
> to learn. Do you know one or two people who deal with this that I could talk
> to for 20 minutes? Happy to send you a short blurb to forward.

**Forwardable blurb:**

> [Your name] is researching how [role] handle [problem]. Not selling
> anything, just wants 20 minutes to hear how you do it today. OK if I connect
> you?

## 2. Cold email

> **Subject:** Question about [problem] at [company]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [name], working on [problem area]. I saw [specific, true reason you
> picked them: a post, a review, their role]. We're early and not selling
> anything; I'm trying to understand how [role] deal with [problem] today.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes in the next two weeks to tell me how you handle
> it? If not, no problem at all.
>
> Thanks,
> [name] | [one line: who you are, a link]

## 3. Cold DM (LinkedIn, community, messaging app)

> Hi [name], I noticed [specific reason]. I'm researching how [role] handle
> [problem] and would value 15-20 minutes of your experience. Not a sales
> call. Open to it?

## 4. Community post

Check the group's rules first and ask a moderator if unsure.

> **Title:** How do you handle [problem]? (researching, not selling)
>
> I'm trying to understand how [role] deal with [problem]. When it last
> happened to you, what did you do, and what did it cost you in time or money?
>
> If you'd be willing to talk for 20 minutes, reply or DM me. I'll share a
> summary of what I learn with the group.

## 5. Follow-up (once, 3-5 days later)

> Hi [name], just bringing this back up in case it got buried. Still keen to
> hear how you handle [problem], 20 minutes whenever suits. If it's not a
> fit, no worries and I won't follow up again.

## 6. After the interview

> Thanks for your time today, [name]. The point about [specific thing they
> said] was really useful. You mentioned [person]; would you be OK introducing
> us? [If promised: here is the summary / resource I mentioned.]
