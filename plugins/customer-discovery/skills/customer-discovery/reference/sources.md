# Sources and credits

This skill summarises ideas from the following works in its own words. Read the
originals; they go much deeper than any summary.

| Idea used here | Source |
|---|---|
| Customer discovery and validation; earlyvangelists | Steve Blank, *The Four Steps to the Epiphany*; Steve Blank and Bob Dorf, *The Startup Owner's Manual* |
| Asking about past behaviour, not opinions; commitment currencies | Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test* |
| Lean canvas | Ash Maurya, *Running Lean* |
| Build-measure-learn; pivot or persevere | Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup* |
| Assumption mapping; experiment catalogue | David J. Bland and Alexander Osterwalder, *Testing Business Ideas* |
| Jobs-to-be-Done | Clayton Christensen et al., *Competing Against Luck* |
| Switch interview; four forces; timeline | Bob Moesta and Chris Spiek; Bob Moesta, *Demand-Side Sales 101* |
| The "very disappointed" survey | Sean Ellis; Rahul Vohra's write-up of the Superhuman PMF engine |
| Price sensitivity meter | Peter van Westendorp |

Thresholds quoted in this skill (5-10 interviews to saturation, 40% very
disappointed, the example pass thresholds) are rules of thumb from practice.
Treat them as starting points, and set your own from what your business needs.
