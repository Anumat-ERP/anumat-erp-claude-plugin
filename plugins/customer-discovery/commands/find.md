Build an outreach plan to reach 30-50 people in: **$ARGUMENTS**

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/customer-discovery/reference/finding-people.md`.
If no ICP exists yet, say so and suggest running `/customer-discovery:icp`
first, or state the segment assumptions you are using.

Ask where the team already has connections (former employers, investors,
advisors, communities) before proposing cold routes.

## Produce, in order

**1. Warm intro ladder.** For this segment, name concrete routes at each
rung: people the team knows, direct intros, second-degree intros, communities,
cold. Estimate how many names each route could give.

**2. Where the segment gathers.** Specific kinds of communities, associations,
events, tool user groups, review sites, and job boards to look in. Where you
cannot name real ones with confidence, describe what to search for rather than
inventing names.

**3. Local routes**, if the market is small or relationship-driven: in-person
visits, local business associations, chambers of commerce, suppliers and
accountants who serve the segment, and the messaging apps people really use.

**4. Messages.** Adapt
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/customer-discovery/templates/outreach-messages.md`
to this segment: a warm intro request, a cold email, a community post, and a
follow-up. Short, honest about stage, asking for learning, not a sale.

**5. Targets and tracking.** How many messages per channel this week, the
tracking table, and the reminder to ask every interviewee for two intros.

**6. When to stop.** Explain saturation (patterns usually repeat after about
5-10 interviews per segment; a heuristic) and what to do if every interview
tells a different story.

## Then stop

Suggest `/customer-discovery:interview` to prepare the guide.
